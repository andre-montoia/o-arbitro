# O Árbitro — Asset Manifest & Prompts

This document contains the specifications and prompts for generating all visual elements for "O Árbitro".

## 1. App Icon
**Objective:** A premium, recognizable icon that represents "The Referee" and "Party Gaming".

**Prompt:**
> "A premium app icon for 'O Árbitro'. Features a stylized, futuristic golden whistle floating in the center of a deep indigo hexagonal plate. The plate has a subtle frosted glass (glassmorphism) texture. Neon purple and hot pink light trails orbit the whistle. 3D render, high-detail, octane render, dramatic cinematic lighting, dark background, 1024x1024, minimalist but luxurious."
